## 生成提示词

### 1. 长曝光星轨

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: candidate photographic background for a slim horizontal banner in a creative workspace app. Generate ONE wide panoramic image with approximately 3:1 aspect ratio, a real-looking long exposure photograph of the night sky with fine curved concentric star trails, their rotation center situated toward the right third. Restrained charcoal and midnight blue sky, subtle natural warm and cool variations in the thin trails, credible astrophotography with delicate sensor grain. Left 40 percent of the image has fewer, much dimmer trails and calm dark negative space for white interface text; main trails concentrated in the middle-right, leaving the far-right button area reasonably quiet. Composition should still work when cropped to a very shallow 10:1 banner. Sky only, no ground or mountains. Quiet and elegant rather than dramatic, modest brightness and low saturation, clearly photographic instead of line-art or computer rendering. No page mockup, no UI, no words, no logos, no exaggerated neon trails, no purple glow, no planets, no aurora, no huge stars.

### 2. 淡银河

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: candidate photographic background for a slim horizontal banner in a creative workspace app. Generate ONE wide panoramic image with approximately 3:1 aspect ratio, a real-looking deep night sky astrophotograph with a faint Milky Way band running diagonally across the middle-right, composed of innumerable minute stars and subtle dusty mottled detail. Charcoal-black and softly slate-blue tones, faint ivory luminosity, natural photographic sensor texture. The left 40 percent is very quiet nearly dark sky with only a few small stars, suitable for white interface copy; the Milky Way is placed in the middle-right, with subdued far-right space for a UI button. Composition must remain attractive after a very shallow 10:1 crop through the center. Sky only. Understated, realistic, restrained contrast, no brightly colored nebulae, no purple, no candy gradients, no landscape, no moon or planets, no aurora, no text or logos, no UI mockup, no illustration.

### 3. 自然星空

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: candidate photographic background for a slim horizontal banner in a creative workspace app. Generate ONE wide panoramic image with approximately 3:1 aspect ratio, a realistic clear starry night sky photo. Fine naturally uneven scattering of small stars on a charcoal and deep slate-blue sky, a few gently brighter stars concentrated in the middle-right, with very subtle warm ivory and cool blue star colors. Delicate real photographic texture and quiet dark atmospheric depth, no visible Milky Way band, no long exposure trails. Left 40 percent has very sparse dim stars and smooth dark negative space for white interface text, far-right area also subdued for a button. Image should work when center-cropped into a very shallow 10:1 banner. Sky only, sophisticated low-saturation treatment, modest contrast. No constellation lines, no landscape, no mountains, no planets or moon, no aurora, no purple glow, no neon, no artificial symmetrical pattern, no huge glitter shapes, no words, no UI or logos.
